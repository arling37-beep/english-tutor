Sample Dialogue — Client Meeting

Tax Advisor: Good morning. I'd like to go over your allowable deductions for this fiscal year.


Client: Great. What expenses can I actually deduct?


Tax Advisor: Any expense that is ordinary and necessary for your business activity qualifies. This includes operational costs, depreciation of assets, and professional service fees.


Client: What about my home office?


Tax Advisor: Yes, if the space is used exclusively for business purposes, you may claim a proportional deduction based on square footage.
